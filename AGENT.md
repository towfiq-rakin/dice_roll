This is a Dice Roll App. There are two players. Each player gets a turn to roll the dice, 
and gets a random point from 1 to 6. The player who reaches the 30 point first, wins the game.

### Functionality
- A simple login and signup using firebase.
- Dice Roll game for two players. 
- After a match finishes, roll again resets the points and starts a new game.

### UI
- In login, signup page, a Dice Icon in middle top.
- After login, the app will be divided into two halfs, upper fro player 2, lower for player 1.
- In each half, at bottom, there will be a screen wide roll dice button. On top of that, there will be total point card on left side, and a dice in right side.
- the Dice will be animated, meaning Roll dice button will trigger to show rolling animation then a dice face, to show the point.
